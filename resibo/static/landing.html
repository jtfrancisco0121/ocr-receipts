8 of the 15 checks

  1. TOTAL_MISMATCHThe total does not equal the sales, VAT and any discount added up.Error
  2. VAT_MISMATCHThe VAT is not 12% of the vatable sales.Review
  3. LINE_ITEMS_MISMATCHThe line items do not add up to the document totals.Review
  4. DUPLICATEThe same invoice number from the same seller appears earlier in this batch.Error
  5. TIN_FORMATThe seller TIN does not have 9, 12 or 14 digits.Review
  6. MISSING_REQUIREDA field this type of document needs is missing.Error or ReviewFor invoices and official receipts a missing seller name, invoice number, invoice date or total is an Error; other missing fields are a Review.
  7. FUTURE_DATEThe invoice date is in the future.Review
  8. LOW_CONFIDENCEThe reader was unsure about some fields. Please check them.Review

How it reads

Upload a PDF. Resibo hands its pages to Claude, which reads every invoice and receipt on them, then checks each result against 15 rules before it comes back as a table, an Excel file or a CSV.

To skip the upload, drop a PDF into a Google Drive folder: an n8n workflow sends it to the API, appends the rows to a Google Sheet and emails a summary. See the n8n guide.

curl -X POST https://<your-host>/api/extract \
  -H "X-API-Key: YOUR_KEY" \
  -F "file=@invoices.pdf"
